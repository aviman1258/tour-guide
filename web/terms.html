What Deodapper is

Deodapper plans a self-guided driving day between two places, around the things you're interested in, and narrates it out loud as you drive. The stops and the stories are written on demand by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) from public sources such as Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap. Deodapper is run by an individual in the United States, not a company with a support desk, so please read this page before you pay.

Free and paid

Drive a saved route is free: pick a route someone else created, set your own day and time, save it to your phone and drive it. Create your own route is paid per route. There is no subscription and no account.

How payment works

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you press Pay, the price shown is charged to your card and appears on your statement, at first as pending. Your route is then planned. If planning fails, or you cancel before it finishes, the charge is cancelled automatically and the pending amount disappears from your statement within a few days; you are not charged for a route you didn't get. Enter an email on the payment form if you'd like Stripe to send you a receipt. Your card details go to Stripe directly and never reach Deodapper; we keep only a payment reference.

Refunds

  • You were not charged for anything that didn't produce a route. That is the normal case for a failed or cancelled plan, and it happens automatically.
  • Delivered routes are not refundable as a rule, because each one is generated for you on demand and costs us to make. The stops are based on public information and may not match your taste; that is not a defect.
  • We will refund a duplicate or accidental purchase, and a route that was genuinely unusable (for example, the planner returned no stops or the narration was missing) if you tell us within 7 days. Contact us with the date of the payment and the start and end of the route. Refunds go back to the original card and can take 5 to 10 days to appear.

Use it sensibly

You are the driver. Deodapper is not a navigation system: the turn banner and spoken directions are a convenience, and your car's or phone's navigation should lead. Obey traffic laws, keep your eyes on the road, and set up the phone before you move off. Stop information, opening hours and stories come from an AI model working from public sources and can be wrong, out of date or incomplete; check anything that matters before you rely on it. Deodapper is provided as is, and our liability to you is limited to the amount you paid for the route in question.

Publishing a route

If you publish a route, you allow other Deodapper users to load and drive it, and you confirm that the title and description you wrote are yours to share. We filter published text for markup, code and abusive language, and we may remove a route at any time. Don't publish addresses that identify a private home; the app refuses obvious ones.

Availability

Planning depends on outside services (Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap routing, the AI model). They are sometimes slow or unavailable, and so, at those moments, is Deodapper. We don't promise any particular uptime. Drive mode works offline once a route is saved to your phone.
